# Income Evidence Triage

## 中文

对赚钱案例、项目数据或平台截图做证据分级，并据此决定只记录、做小实验还是扩大投入。

适用于判断项目是否真赚钱、播放量或注册能否证明需求；不用于完整账目审计。曝光、演示和注册都不能替代付款、复购与利润证据。

## English

Classify evidence in income claims, project metrics, or platform screenshots, then decide whether to simply record the lead, run a small experiment, or scale investment.

Use it to assess whether a project truly earns money or whether views and sign-ups demonstrate demand. It is not a full financial audit. Impressions, demos, and registrations do not replace evidence of payment, repeat purchases, or profit.
